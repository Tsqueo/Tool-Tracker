The Load-out Challenge

5:00

Plan the load. Make the stops.

Choose a truck and loading position for each item. Balance the weight, keep incompatible loads apart and load in reverse delivery order. This is a load-planning game, not a crew schedule.

Five minutes. Two attempts this week. Leaving pauses the clock. Each new attempt starts fresh; completed results remain available.

Position 1 = loaded first, deepest in the truck. Higher numbers are loaded later, nearest the door. Stop 1 unloads first, so load its items last. Positions must be unique within each truck; gaps are allowed. You may choose either truck.
